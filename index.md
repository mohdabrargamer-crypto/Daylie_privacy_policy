Privacy Policy – Daylie: Daily Routine Tracker

Effective Date: 16 November 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Daylie – Daily Routine Tracker.

Your privacy is important to us. Daylie is designed to help you organize your daily activities, build healthy habits, manage routines, and track your personal progress while keeping your information private.

This Privacy Policy explains how Daylie handles, stores, and protects information when you use the application.

By using Daylie, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Daylie is designed with privacy in mind.

The application stores information locally on your device to provide its features. This may include:

- Daily routines and scheduled tasks.
- Task completion records and outcomes.
- Task categories, priorities, and durations.
- Focus session information.
- Daily schedules and personal preferences.
- Reminder and notification preferences.
- App appearance settings, including light and dark mode.

You do not need to create an account or provide personal registration information to use the application.

3. How Your Information Is Used

Information stored by Daylie is used to provide and improve your experience within the application.

It helps the app to:

- Organize and manage your daily routines.
- Schedule tasks and activities.
- Track completed and pending tasks.
- Monitor routine consistency and progress.
- Display productivity insights and statistics.
- Provide personalized suggestions based on your activity patterns.
- Send reminders for scheduled activities.
- Help you understand your daily and weekly performance.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your privacy is a priority.

Daylie stores routines, tasks, completion records, focus sessions, and preferences locally on your device.

The app's insights and suggestions are calculated on your device.

Daylie does not upload this information to our servers.

There is no cloud synchronization or mandatory account registration.

You are responsible for keeping your device secure and maintaining backups of your information when needed.

5. Notifications and Reminders

Daylie may use your device's notification functionality to remind you about scheduled activities and routines.

You can manage reminder preferences within the application.

You can also disable notifications through your device settings.

Notifications are intended to help you stay organized and maintain consistency with your daily activities.

6. Health and Fitness-Related Activities

Daylie allows users to organize and track personal activities, including exercise routines and other wellness-related habits.

The application may record whether you have completed a scheduled exercise activity.

Daylie is a routine and productivity tracking application, not a medical application.

It does not provide medical diagnoses, treatment recommendations, or professional healthcare advice.

7. Data Sharing and Third Parties

Daylie is designed to keep your personal routine information on your device.

We do not sell, rent, or share your locally stored routine information with third parties.

The application does not upload your routine records or personal productivity insights to external servers.

If future versions introduce third-party services or additional data-processing features, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Your information remains stored locally on your device until you choose to remove it or uninstall the application.

Daylie provides a delete-all-data option within the Profile section.

You can also use the available backup and restore functionality to manage your app data.
